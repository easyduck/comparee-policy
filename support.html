Using Comparee

Comparee works out the price per unit and tells you which product is the better buy.

  1. Type the quantity and the price for item A.
  2. Do the same for item B.
  3. The cheaper one is highlighted and the verdict appears underneath. There is no Calculate button — it updates as you type.

Entering numbers quickly

  • → and ← in the bottom bar move between fields without closing the number pad.
  • + adds an item, up to five (A–E). − removes the last one, down to two.
  • Reset clears everything in one tap.
  • Both . and , work as the decimal separator.

Seeing the full ranking

Tap View Results for every product ranked by unit price, with how much more expensive each one is than the winner. The best value shows 0%.

Questions

Does it convert between units?

No, and that is deliberate. Comparee compares the numbers you type, so use matching units — grams with grams, millilitres with millilitres, pieces with pieces. Comparing 500 g against 1 kg without converting first will give you a misleading answer.

Why does it say "Same Value"?

Because the unit prices really are equal. Comparee will not pick a winner at random when there isn't one.

The percentages look inconsistent

They measure two different things, and both are correct. On the main screen, "B is 10% cheaper than A" is calculated against A's price. In the ranking, "A +11%" is calculated against B's price. The same pair of products gives 10% and 11% depending on which one you measure from.

How do I change the language?

Tap the gear icon, then Language. Choose System, English, or ไทย. It applies immediately, no restart needed.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every comparison feature works with no connection. Only the ads need the network, and the app is fully usable without them.

Ads and privacy

Comparee is free and supported by ads. The numbers you type never leave your device. If your region requires it, Settings shows a Privacy options entry where you can change or withdraw advertising consent at any time. Full detail is in the privacy policy.

Reporting a wrong result

Please email the exact quantities and prices you entered and what you expected. The calculation uses decimal arithmetic specifically to avoid rounding surprises, so a genuine mismatch is worth investigating.

  1. กรอกปริมาณและราคาของสินค้า A

กรอกตัวเลขให้เร็ว

  • → และ ← ในแถบล่างเลื่อนช่องกรอกโดยไม่ปิดแป้นตัวเลข
  • + เพิ่มรายการได้ถึงห้ารายการ (A–E) − ลดได้ถึงสองรายการ
  • ล้างค่า เคลียร์ทุกอย่างในหนึ่งแตะ
  • ใช้ทั้ง . และ , เป็นจุดทศนิยมได้
